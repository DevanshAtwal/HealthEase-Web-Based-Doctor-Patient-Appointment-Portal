About HealthEase

HealthEase is a web-based appointment portal designed to bridge the gap between patients and doctors. Patients can register, search for doctors by speciality, location, or consultation fees, and book appointments instantly. Doctors can manage their schedules, view appointments, and cancel or reschedule when needed. The admin panel ensures everything runs smoothly and securely.
